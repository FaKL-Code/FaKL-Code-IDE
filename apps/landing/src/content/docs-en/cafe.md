---
titulo: 'Cyber café'
descricao: "The agents' room in pixel art."
ordem: 11
---

Open it with **Open the cyber café**. The room is drawn in isometric pixel art, and each live agent is an avatar sitting at a computer.

- **Thumbnails:** each agent's monitor shows what it is doing, such as the file it is editing, the command it is running, or the tests.
- **States:** speech bubbles show when the agent is thinking, waiting for a permission, or has failed.
- **Breaks:** when it finishes the task, the agent gets up, celebrates, and heads to the water cooler, the coffee, the arcade, or the pool table, sometimes to chat with another agent.
- **Daily scoreboard:** tasks, PRs, and cost, the same as on the Desk.
- **Time of day:** the room's light follows the clock. After a while idle, someone dozes off and the cat climbs into their lap.

Clicking an avatar opens its conversation. The café also shows up on your phone and can be moved to another monitor.
