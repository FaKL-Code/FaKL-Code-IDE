## The canvas

Open it with **Open the Desk**. Each session is a card with the name, provider, state, worktree branch, and a usage ring. Wires connect the Maestro to its subagents. From the card you chat, answer permission requests, see the changes, and open the log or the terminal.

States change on screen as soon as the event arrives: preparing, thinking, running, waiting for permission, done, stopped, or failed.

## Focus

**Desk in focus** hides the IDE's bars and leaves only the canvas.

## Other monitors

The Desk, agents chat, cyber café, editor, and terminal can move to windows on other monitors, with the **… in another window (other monitor)** commands. When you reopen the IDE, they return to where they were. **Bring back to the main window** undoes it.

## Minimized mode

**Minimized mode** collapses the IDE and leaves a pet and an agents panel on top of all windows, with states, decisions, and permissions. A tray icon and a global shortcut bring the IDE back.
